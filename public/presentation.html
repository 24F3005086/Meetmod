The Participation Imbalance Problem

Conversational Monopoly
In virtual classrooms or discussions, statistically 20% of dominant voices occupy over 80% of talking time. This forces quieter students to retreat into passivity (the free-rider dilemma).
Platform Passivity
Existing software (Zoom, Teams, Meet) are passive communication streams. They lack dynamic tools to track individual participation rates or intervene algorithmically in real-time.

The Solution: MeetMod

An active, analytical web platform built to balance group discussions.

Real-Time Metrics
Tracks exact speaking duration, percentage distribution, and inter-peer interruptions on a live sidebar dashboard.
Active Moderation
Automatically prompts warning cues and applies temporary forced-mutes to monopolizing speakers using an engine rule loop.
Quiet Nudges
Detects inactive students who have been silent for extended windows and pushes friendly alerts encouraging contribution.

Algorithmic Rigor: The Gini Index

Instead of standard deviation, we adopt the economic **Gini Coefficient** ($G$) to evaluate how evenly conversation time is distributed among the $n$ participants.

G =
∑i=1n ∑j=1n |ti - tj| 2n ∑i=1n ti
  • G = 0.00: Perfect conversation equality.
  • G = 1.00: Complete monopoly (1 speaker).
User-Facing Equity Score

To make this highly readable, the platform displays the **Equity Score**:

Score = (1 - G) × 100

Displayed in a circular progress ring on both the live dashboard and the final session report dashboard.

Interruption Collision Engine

  • Overlap Detection: When Client B fires a speaking-start signal while Client A is active, the server detects a collision.
  • Dynamic Increments:
    • Interrupter receives +1 to Given metrics.
    • Active speaker receives +1 to Received metrics.
  • Visual Evidence: Immediately logs to the meeting's public timeline (e.g. "Student A interrupted Professor").

AI-Powered Interventions

Configurable rule thresholds applied recursively on the server-side update loop:

1. Warning Alert
Triggers after 120s of continuous monologue. Alerts speaker with a toast popup.
2. Countdown
Triggers at 180s. A high-intensity full screen overlay forces visual focus.
3. Force Mute
Triggers at 200s. Mutes local audio capture via peer tracks on the client.
4. Inactive Nudge
Identifies lowest speak percentage and prompts to invite their insights.

Lobby Waiting Room (Admission Control)

  • Entry Gating: Non-host attendees are suspended in a waiting room overlay upon joining.
  • Interactive Toasts: Host receives immediate notifications with dedicated Admit and Decline controls.
  • Device Security: Client-side media constraints (`getUserMedia`) are bypassed and locked until approved, preventing accidental mic/camera leaks in the lobby.
  • Ghost Request Prevention: Socket disconnect handles removal of knocking users who leave early, automatically canceling active toasts.
