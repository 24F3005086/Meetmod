The Participation Imbalance Problem

Virtual learning environments suffer from structural inequities during open group discussions.

Conversational Monopoly
In virtual classrooms, statistically 20% of dominant voices occupy over **80% of talking time**. This forces quieter students to retreat into passivity—creating a "free-rider" learning dilemma.
Platform Passivity
Existing software like Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet are passive channels. They do not track individual participation distribution or intervene algorithmically to balance speech time.

The Solution: MeetMod

An active, analytical meeting environment built to balance dialogue and engagement.

Real-Time Metrics
Calculates and renders exact speaking duration, percentage distribution, and peer-to-peer interruption dynamics on a live sidebar dashboard.
Active Moderation
Enforces server-guided warning triggers, full screen overlays, and automatic audio track disables for monopolizing speakers to restore conversational balance.
Quiet Nudging
Detects silent members who have not contributed and fires friendly notifications to invite their unique insights to the topic.

Algorithmic Rigor: The Gini Index

To quantify conversation equality mathematically, we repurpose the economic **Gini Coefficient** ($G$), a measure of statistical dispersion:

G =
∑i=1n ∑j=1n |ti - tj| 2n ∑i=1n ti
  • G = 0.00: Complete equity (everyone speaks exactly the same).
  • G = 1.00: Complete inequality (one speaker dominates).
User-Facing Equity Score

For intuitive real-time tracking, this is converted to an **Equity Score**:

Equity Score = (1 - G) × 100

Rendered on a circular progress ring on the live dashboard and the final session report.

AI-Powered Interventions

Configurable rule thresholds evaluated recursively on the active speaking update loop:

1
Warning Alert
Monologue time reaches 120s. Shows a toast alert suggesting wrap-up.
2
Countdown
Monologue reaches 180s. Triggers a full-screen countdown overlay.
3
Force Mute
Monologue reaches 200s. Client mutes local audio and notifies the room.
4
Inactive Nudge
Analyzes speaking times and pushes gentle nudges to encourage participation.

Lobby Waiting Room (Admission Control)

  • Entry Gating: Non-host attendees are suspended in a glassmorphic waiting screen upon joining.
  • Interactive Alerts: Host receives real-time admission prompts containing **Admit** and **Decline** actions.
  • Strict Device Privacy: Browser camera/microphone queries (`getUserMedia`) are bypassed and locked until approved, preventing accidental media leaks.
  • Cleanup Handler: If a user leaves the lobby early, the server cancels the host's toast popup automatically.
